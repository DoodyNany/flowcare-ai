Safety rules built into the prototype

Clinicians decideEvery suggestion and warning needs a human decision, and the decision is logged.
ExplainableEvery output shows why: factor bars, heat maps and forecast inputs.
Privacy firstNo real patient data. In a hospital, data stays on site under Saudi PDPL.
No early dischargeOnly patients a doctor already cleared are suggested.
Runs offlineAll models run on this computer: no cloud, no outside AI.
Full audit logEvery action and decision is recorded and can be exported.
Safety-first thresholdsA missed case costs more than an extra review.
